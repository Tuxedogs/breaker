question: "Which capital hull do we kill first?"

criteria:
  - label: Damage potential
    weight: high
    description: Primary armament type and fire rate relative to your hull class
  - label: Relative position
    weight: high
    description: Exposure to your weapons vs exposure to theirs
  - label: Engineering presence
    weight: med
    description: Active repair capability extends effective hull life significantly

matrix:
  - condition: Single capital hull present
    action: Assign all fire. No triage needed.
  - condition: Two hulls, unequal threat
    action: Primary kill on higher damage potential. Secondary pressure on the other.
  - condition: Two hulls, equal threat
    action: Assign by position. Kill the one your weapons already bear on.
  - condition: Hull disabled mid-engagement
    action: Re-evaluate order. Do not continue damage on a non-threat.

output:
  - label: Primary kill target
    description: Full damage commitment until disabled
  - label: Secondary pressure target
    description: Maintain tracking position, do not fully commit

failureModes:
  - Target swaps with no comms handoff
  - Undervaluing enemy engineering presence
