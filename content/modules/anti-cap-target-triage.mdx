Triage is not about which ship is scariest — it's about which ship
your weapons can realistically reach right now. A Javelin at range
with its broadside away from you scores lower than an Idris already
turned toward your formation, even if the Javelin is nominally the
bigger threat.

Engineering presence is the most commonly underweighted factor.
A hull with active repair can absorb your damage output indefinitely.
If you see support ships docked or running parallel, adjust your
damage potential score upward accordingly.
