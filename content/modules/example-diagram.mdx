The staggered line keeps all three ships in a mutual support posture while minimizing the number of guns masked by friendly hulls. Lead calls range and commit. Wings hold position offset by roughly 0.3 km to preserve their own fire solutions.

The dead zone behind the formation is not a weakness — it is intentional. Trying to cover the rear inverts the formation geometry and destroys the primary arc overlap. Use dedicated picket ships for rear coverage, not the formation itself.
