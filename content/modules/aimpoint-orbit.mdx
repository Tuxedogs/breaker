## Core Idea

Aimpoint orbit is the pilot's geometry job. The goal is to stay aft of the target,
hold a stable angle on the intended subsystem, and avoid movement that breaks gunner
tracking windows.

## What Good Looks Like

- The target remains in a predictable firing lane for your gunners.
- Your nose stays disciplined around the intended component or quadrant.
- Thrust corrections are deliberate and small instead of reactive and sweeping.

## Common Breaks

- Oversteering to chase the target's hull instead of preserving the slot.
- Lateral thrust corrections that mask turret shots.
- Boosting without a purpose and collapsing the range too fast.

> This concept is about geometry control. Target selection still belongs to the
> crew and should flow from Component Sniping or higher-level target triage.
