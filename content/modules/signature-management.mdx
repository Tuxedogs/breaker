## Signature Reference

| Signature | What Drives It | Why The Crew Cares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EM | Power allocation, active systems such as shields, thrusters and QT drives | Passive detectability and lock windows, especially during setup and withdrawal |
| IR | Heat state, sustained boosting, prolonged output, on board fires | Expands your visibility and can punish extended high-output posture |
| CS | Physical presence, active radar detection and evasion | Can be managed with good habits, but geometry still changes how easy you are to classify |

## Baseline Rules

- Reduce power allocation of shields to online minimums if evading detection.
- Coordinate power changes on comms so pilot and engineer do not create accidental lock windows.
- Posture with your lowest cross section visible towards the enemy, for most hulls, nose-on.
