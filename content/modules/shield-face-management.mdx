## Core Idea

Shield faces are directional survivability pools. The crew's job is not to watch
them break in sequence. The crew's job is to decide which face should be taking
pressure right now and rotate away before that answer becomes the worst possible one.

## Practical Reads

- A weak face is a geometry problem, not just an engineering problem.
- Over-rotation is often worse than holding slightly too long.
- A clean call like "front face soft" should trigger a controlled presentation change,
  not a panic maneuver.

## Crew Discipline

- Pilots manage presentation.
- Engineers call face state early enough to matter.
- Gunners report whether the required rotation is breaking their firing lane.

> Good shield management buys time. Bad shield management spends time you do not have.
