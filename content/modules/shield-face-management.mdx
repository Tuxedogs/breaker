## Core Idea

Shield faces are directional survivability pools. The crew's job is not to watch
them break in sequence. The crew's job is to decide which face should be taking
pressure and which ones still require additional recovery windows.

## Practical Reads

- Over-rotation is often worse than holding slightly too long.
- A clean call like "rolling shields" should adequately inform gunners to prepare for adjusting fire,
  not a panic maneuver.

## Crew Discipline

- Pilots mitigate damage.
- Gunners report whether the required rotation is breaking their firing lane.

> Good shield management buys time, a lot of time. Bad shield management reduces both defensive and offensive capability.
