steps:
  - label: Identify, defending against certain missile types varies. 
    detail: Callout, missile locked and launch vs incoming.
  - label: Use decoys H(flares) for incoming ordnance.
    detail: Search for missile track on radar or visual, callout launch heading/altitude.
  - label: Use noise (chaff) to break enemy target locking.
    detail: Noise is for target denial and lock disruption, it does not aid in defeating missiles that are already committed.
  - label: Signatures
    detail: Dumping signature is highly effective in lowering the expenditure of flares required to defeat incoming.
  - label: Posture ship against probable hits.
    detail: Align the strongest aspect of your ship with the track of the missile/torpedo to minimize hull damage.

failureModes:
  - Panic dumping early leaves no protection for follow-up volleys.
  - Crew treats noise (chaff) like a substitute for timed decoys (flares).
  - Crew overcommits while low on countermeasure inventory.
