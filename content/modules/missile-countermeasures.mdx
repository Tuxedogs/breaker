steps:
  - label: Classify the threat
    detail: Read the incoming cadence and lock state before spending inventory.
  - label: Use decoys (flares) for incoming ordnance
    detail: Release decoys in measured bursts when the threat is already inbound and tracking.
  - label: Use noise (chaff) to break or complicate targeting
    detail: Noise is for target denial and lock disruption, not for magically deleting a missile that is already committed.
  - label: Do not dump everything early
    detail: Preserve enough inventory for the follow-up volley and report remaining state on comms.
  - label: Adjust the ship plan
    detail: If inventory is getting low, change commit, extension, or support posture before the next wave arrives.

failureModes:
  - Panic dumping early leaves no protection for follow-up volleys
  - Crew treats noise (chaff) like a substitute for timed decoys (flares)
  - Crew overcommits while low on countermeasure inventory
