Shield faces come in two varieties:

Bubble -  which means the shield takes damage no matter what side you're hit from.

Defensively rolling shield faces means rotating your ship so incoming fire is spread across multiple shield quadrants instead of allowing one face to collapse under sustained pressure. 
By cycling fresh faces into the attack angle, you exploit shield damage delay recharge mechanics, since recently untouched faces can begin recovering while the enemy continues wasting shots on stronger coverage. 
The priority is preventing any shield face from fully depleting, because once a face drops, the damage-down recharge delay is typically longer and recovery becomes slower, leaving you exposed at the exact moment things are already going badly.
