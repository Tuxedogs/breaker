question: "What gets restored before a multicrew ship recommits?"
criteria:
  - label: Exit the kill lane if able
    weight: high
    description: Survive first. Break LOS of high damage enemies and assess situation.
  - label: Stabilize ongoing damage conditions
    weight: high
    description: Firefighting takes immediate priority. In some cases even the bridge crew will change to firefighting.
  - label: Recover survivability, once fires are controlled
    weight: med
    description: Stabilize power plants, shields so the ship can effectively defend itself.
  - label: Restore weapons by mission value
    weight: med
    description: Restore enough functionality to complete mission or defend itself to port, not every damaged subsystem.
  - label: Recommit only on crew confirmation
    weight: high
    description: Pilot, gunners, and engineer confirm readiness before the ship turns back in.
matrix:
  - condition: Still inside concentrated fire
    action: Extend farther. Recovery does not start until the ship can stop bleeding.
  - condition: Mobility degraded
    action: Restore escape and orientation control before weapon systems.
  - condition: Weapons degraded but survivability stable
    action: Recover the minimum weapon package needed for the next task.
  - condition: Crew status unclear
    action: Hold outside the fight and force a readiness check before recommit.
output:
  - label: Recovery state
    description: Crew can state whether the ship is extending, stabilizing, or ready.
  - label: Recommit gate
    description: The ship turns back in only after explicit cross-crew confirmation.

This is a multicrew discipline rule, not a Perseus-only emergency script.
