  - label: Crew
    items: [All seats confirmed occupied, Engineer confirms power mode, Gunners confirm targeting mode, FC confirms everyone on comms]
  - label: Weapons
    items: [Cycle all weapons online, Confirm targeting mode is active, Check missile payload count, Verify decoy loadout]
  - label: Navigation
    items: [Confirm approach vector, Mark primary and fallback exit, Identify hardpoints on primary target, Note nearest cover]
---

This checklist is not optional on first engagement. It becomes optional only after the crew has run it enough times that it is automatic.

The most commonly skipped phase is Navigation. Pilots focus on weapons and power and enter an engagement without a fallback exit identified. When the fight goes wrong, they improvise under fire instead of executing a pre-planned break.
