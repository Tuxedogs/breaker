criteria:
  - label: Fire suppression
    weight: high
    description: Fires cascade into ship loss. Put them out before any other repair work.
  - label: Power plants
    weight: high
    description: Restore the systems that keep the ship alive, steerable, and coordinated.
  - label: Shields and survivability layers
    weight: med
    description: Recover the systems that let the ship survive the next exchange.
  - label: Primary weapons systems
    weight: med
    description: Hull survival is priority, provide advantages for friendlies still applying damage.
matrix:
  - condition: Active fires present
    action: Suppress them immediately. No exceptions.
  - condition: Power supply is crippled
    action: Restore ship power prioritize survival before offensive capability.
  - condition: Primary weapon disabled
    action: Turret operator and Engineer repair weapon system until priority shift.
  - condition: Crew wants to recommit early
    action: Verify ship viability with engineering and make determination
output:
  - label: Repair order
    description: Everyone can state the same top priority without debate.
  - label: Last Stand
    description: Ship is no longer capapable of providing offensive assistance and all crew shift to active repairs.

This ladder is a hard priority rule, not a suggestion.
