question: "What gets repaired first when multiple things are broken?"
criteria:
  - label: Fire suppression
    weight: high
    description: Fires cascade into ship loss. Put them out before any other repair work.
  - label: Power and command-critical systems
    weight: high
    description: Restore the systems that keep the ship alive, steerable, and coordinated.
  - label: Shields and survivability layers
    weight: med
    description: Recover the systems that let the ship survive the next exchange.
  - label: Mission-value weapons and support systems
    weight: med
    description: Repair the systems required for the next task before low-impact extras.
matrix:
  - condition: Active fires present
    action: Suppress them immediately. No exceptions.
  - condition: Mobility or power is degraded
    action: Restore ship control and survival before offensive capability.
  - condition: Weapons degraded but ship stable
    action: Repair the smallest set needed to return mission value.
  - condition: Crew wants to recommit early
    action: Hold until critical systems are restored and acknowledged on comms.
output:
  - label: Repair order
    description: Everyone can state the same top priority without debate.
  - label: Recommit gate
    description: The ship does not turn back in while the ladder is still unresolved.
---

This ladder is a hard priority rule, not a suggestion.
