useWhen:
  - Before every atmospheric entry.
  - During emergency descent from orbit.
  - When transitioning from quantum travel to low-orbit engagement.

steps:
  - label: Confirm heading
    detail: Verify entry angle is within the safe descent corridor before committing thrust.
  - label: Set shield face
    detail: Bring shield coverage to full front facing before hitting the boundary layer.
  - label: Cut quantum drive
    detail: Decouple the QT drive and transition to SCM speed before the atmospheric boundary.
  - label: Announce descent
    detail: Call out descent initiation on comms so the crew can brace and gunners hold fire.
  - label: Monitor hull heat
    detail: Watch hull heat during high-angle entries and extend to bleed speed if approaching threshold.
  - label: Confirm SCM lock
    detail: Verify you are fully transitioned to SCM before attempting any weapons fire or maneuvering.

failureModes:
  - Entering with wrong shield alignment exposes full hull to ground-based fire.
  - No comms callout causes collision risk with other elements entering simultaneously.
  - Overshooting the descent corridor forces a full re-orbit burn.
---

Standard orbital entries are predictable. The risk is not the maneuver itself — it is the crew being unprepared for the transition. Call out before you commit, not after.

Heat management is the most commonly skipped step on high-angle entries. A few seconds of extended approach prevents a full cooldown cycle mid-engagement.
