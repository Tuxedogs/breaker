steps:
  - label: Choose the correct tool
    detail: Use snare for lane pulls and planned interdiction windows. Use dampener for immediate escape denial during an active fight.
  - label: Once established within the lane, charge and fire snare.
    detail: The snare is the QID weapon selector. The physical snare button is on the left pilot seat armrest.
  - label: Once a target is snared, drop snare and activate dampener.
    detail: Fire QID to stop snare, switch to QED and fire dampener. Alternatively use the right armrest switch "Activate dampener" 
  - label: Maintain range at all times 
    detail: The dampener as an approximately 11 km practical tool and stay near 10 km when possible.
  - label: Reconfigure after the event by looping back to charging snare.
    detail: Running multiple interdictors reduces turnaround time on  phases.
failureModes:
  - Crew uses snare when dampener is required for immediate escape denial
  - Tackle ship drifts outside the effective dampener envelope
  - Crew forgets to swap from snare to dampener after the quantum interdiction event
