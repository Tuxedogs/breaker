steps:
  - label: Choose the correct tool
    detail: Use snare for lane pulls and planned interdiction windows. Use dampener for immediate escape denial during an active fight.
  - label: Account for snare charge time
    detail: The snare is the QID weapon selector. The physical snare button is on the left pilot seat armrest.
  - label: Once snared, drop snare and activate dampener.
    detail: Treat the dampener as an approximately 11 km practical tool and stay near 10 km when possible.
  - label: Keep tackle calls explicit
    detail: Call snare charging, snare active, dampener active, and any tails or damage states clearly.
  - label: Reconfigure after the event
    detail: Do not leave the ship in the wrong interdiction state after the pull or chase shifts phases.
failureModes:
  - Crew uses snare when dampener is required for immediate escape denial
  - Tackle ship drifts outside the effective dampener envelope
  - Crew forgets to swap from snare posture to dampener posture after the event

Mantis baseline: snare reaches farther but costs time. Dampener is the immediate fight tool.
