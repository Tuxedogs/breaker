# Wikelo catalog status

As of 2026-10-26, `wikelo_trade_requirements_2026-10-26.csv` is missing. The published catalog is intentionally empty. The 87 mission offers are not a verified trade catalog. The development fixture contains only synthetic 10, 11, and 20 line layout cases and is never published as trade data.

The typed catalog requires each trade's mission title, reward, additional rewards, category, minimum reputation, requirement quantities, and any loadout components to be checked before publication. Source metadata is attached to each trade. Candidate references are [Star Citizen Wiki](https://starcitizen.tools/Wikelo) and [Dumper's Repo](https://dumpers-repo.com/wikelo/); neither supplies the missing approved CSV. The wiki confirms the general Wikelo trade and reputation model, not this catalog's individual rows.

Artwork entries require a local file path, attribution, and verified reuse rights before use. Rights for third party artwork, including RSI and fandom images, remain unresolved. No third party images are packaged or hotlinked; cards use a graphite fallback.

Manual owned amounts and pins are browser-local, versioned Wikelo state. They do not represent inventory or synchronized progress.
